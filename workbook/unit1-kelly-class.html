Hi, my name is Kelly. I'm eleven years old. My classmates are from different countries. No one is from the same country as me.

Andy, Ben, Jack, and Leo are from the USA. They play together all day. Ella is from Australia. She likes koalas, and I like koalas, too. Tracy is from Japan. We have art class together on Friday. We can both draw very well!

老師流程（閱讀策略，4 分鐘）：先問 "How many names can you find?"，點名字會亮起來，再點國家配對。追問：Where is Kelly from? (The text doesn't say! Look at the chart: Taiwan has only 1 student, and no one is from the same country as Kelly.) 這是推論題，讓學生當偵探。生字：different / same / as / together / both。
老師流程（配對遊戲，3 分鐘）：點左邊名字，再點右邊國家。答對變綠，答錯變紅再試。全班一起唸 "Andy is from the USA." 四個美國男孩可以請四位同學站起來當他們。
