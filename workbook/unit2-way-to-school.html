老師流程（TPR 暖身，3 分鐘）：點一張卡，全班一起做動作並大聲說 "by bus"。按 Random 會隨機亮一張，看誰最快說出來。注意 on foot 不是 by foot。
老師流程（圖表閱讀，5 分鐘）：點上面的交通方式，只留那個顏色，請學生數 "How many students go to school by car on Monday?"。點星期幾會顯示該天的數字與天氣。Thursday 的天氣沒寫，請學生從圖表推理："It's windy and cloudy, like Tuesday." 也可以問 "Why do many students walk on Friday?" (It's sunny.)

There are twenty-five students in Molly's class. They go to school in different ways depending on the weather. For example, it's sunny on Monday and Friday. Many students go to school or by bike. It's windy and cloudy on Tuesday. Eight students go to school and students go to school by metro. It's rainy on Wednesday. students go to school by car, and only student goes to school on foot. No one goes to school .
