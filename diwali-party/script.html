網路斷線已經開好的畫面可以繼續用，不要重新整理。簡報裡的 Lunch Box Lab 如果打不開，按下方「另開分頁」。

For Ankita: your lines after 20:00

You lead until 20:00. After that, you are the party host.

  • 20:00 Hand over: "What food should we bring to the party?"
  • Spin Lead the second repeat: "Say it with me."
  • Catch Sit next to each child for about 20 seconds: "Say it with me."
  • Party Menu Ask one group: "What food is from India?" Then: "Yes! I love samosas!"
  • Share After each menu: "Thank you!"
  • Impostor Read the sentence on the screen aloud, mistakes included.
  • Speed Round You are the judge. Repeat the correct sentence: "I want some ___. Great!"
  • At the door Each child says "I want some ___!" You answer: "Here you are! Happy Diwali!"
